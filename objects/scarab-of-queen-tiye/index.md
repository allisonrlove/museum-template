## About this object

The scarab beetle was a symbol of renewal in ancient Egypt, and its shape was a favourite for seals and amulets. This one is different: its flat underside carries a text naming the king and his queen. Copies were made in large numbers and sent out as a kind of royal announcement.

## Looking closely

The beetle is shown simply, with its wing cases and head marked by a few incised lines. The real subject is underneath, where the hieroglyphs fill the oval in tidy rows.

## Why this object is in the collection

It shows how a ruler could use a small, cheap, portable object as media. The same words, repeated on hundreds of scarabs, reached people who would never see the palace.
