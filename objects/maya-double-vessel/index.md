## About this object

Maya potters built vessels from coils of clay and painted them with mineral slips before firing. Lidded vessels like this were placed in tombs and may have contained food, drink or offerings for the dead.

## Looking closely

The two chambers are joined at the sides and covered with a single lid. Patterns of painted bands help read the form as one object while keeping the two chambers visible.

## Why this object is in the collection

It reminds us that a ceramic can be both a household thing and a gift to the dead, and that most of what we know about the Maya past comes from what they chose to bury.
