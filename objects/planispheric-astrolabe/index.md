## About this object

An astrolabe is a two-dimensional map of the heavens. A rotating pointer, called a rete, shows the fixed stars over a plate engraved for the latitude where the instrument is used. Turn it to match a star's height and the time can be read from the scale.

## Looking closely

The rete is pierced into curling shapes so the plate shows through. The beauty is functional: every cut-out leaves a place for the engraved lines beneath to remain visible.

## Why this object is in the collection

It joins mathematics, geography, religion and craft in one handheld object, and shows how scholars across the Islamic world refined an instrument they inherited from Greek astronomy.
