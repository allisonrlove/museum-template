## About this object

A gui was a bronze food vessel used in ceremonies. Families offered grain to ancestors in sets of vessels like this one. Some carry inscriptions inside, recording who made them and why.

## Looking closely

The bowl is supported on a base, with handles at the sides and a band of decoration around the body. Bronze like this was cast in clay moulds, so every line was prepared before the metal was poured.

## Why this object is in the collection

Bronze was costly and political. Owning ritual vessels meant having the right to take part in rituals that defined who belonged to the ruling world.
