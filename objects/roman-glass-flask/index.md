## About this object

Glassblowing was invented around the first century BCE and spread across the Roman world within a few generations. A worker gathered molten glass on a pipe and inflated it, making vessels in minutes that would have taken days to carve from stone.

## Looking closely

The walls are thin and the neck long. Slight irregularities in the surface show it was shaped by breath and hand, not a mould.

## Why this object is in the collection

It is evidence that ordinary people owned glass. What had been a luxury material became a container for oils, perfumes and medicines.
