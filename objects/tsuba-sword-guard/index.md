## About this object

A tsuba is the guard of a Japanese sword. Its job is practical: to stop the hand sliding onto the blade and to balance the weapon. During the long peace of the Edo period, swords were worn more as marks of status than used, and craftsmen competed to decorate their guards.

## Looking closely

Notice the openings cut through the metal and the inlaid pattern around them. Designs often drew on nature, poetry or stories that the owner wanted others to recognise.

It shows how a tool became a statement. A sword guard is a small object that tells a viewer who its owner wanted to be.
