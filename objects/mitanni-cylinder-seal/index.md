## About this object

A cylinder seal is a small stone tube, engraved in reverse, that is rolled across wet clay to leave a continuous picture. Owners used them to close jars, seal letters and confirm agreements. The photograph shows the seal itself on the left and a modern impression on the right.

## Looking closely

The image repeats around the cylinder, so there is no beginning or end: a nude hero, griffins, a monkey, a lion and a goat crowd one another.

## Why this object is in the collection

It was a signature before signatures. Whoever owned it could press a personal mark onto clay, which makes it both an administrative tool and a small work of art.
